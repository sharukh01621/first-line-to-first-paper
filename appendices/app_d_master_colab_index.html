D.2 Spine Public Health Datasets Dictionary

Three realistic, public-health-inspired synthetic datasets accompany this curriculum from Chapter 1 through Chapter 36 so you can trace how the exact same data problems evolve from basic Python loops to calibrated ML and PyTorch models.

D.2.1 1. clinic_triage.csv (120 Rows, 11 Columns)

  • Domain: Upazila community clinic patient intake records across 6 districts.
  • Exact Columns: patient_id (UHC-0001–UHC-0120), district, age_years, sex (F/M), weight_kg, height_cm, temp_c, systolic_bp, hemoglobin_g_dl, fever_days, triage_priority (Routine: 64, Moderate: 56).
  • Data Quality & Quirks: Zero missing cells by design—serves as the clean anchor dataset for Parts I–III (BMI calculation, fever triage branching, CSV/JSON I/O, OOP PatientRecord classes) and introductory classification/regression benchmarks.

D.2.2 2. mch_survey_sample.csv (500 Rows, 12 Columns)

  • Domain: Demographic and Health Survey (DHS)-style Maternal & Child Health household survey spanning 8 divisions and 50 primary sampling clusters (cluster_id).

  • Exact Columns: household_id (MCH-00001–MCH-00500), cluster_id, division, residence, wealth_quintile (Poorest to Richest), maternal_age_years, anc_visits, maternal_hb_g_dl, maternal_anemia (0/1), child_haz, child_stunted (0/1), fully_vaccinated (0/1).

  • Deliberate Real-World Quirks:

    • residence contains mixed-case entry errors ('Rural': 345, 'Urban': 140, 'rural': 11, 'urban': 4) requiring .str.strip().str.title().
    • anc_visits encodes 8 “Don’t Know / Refused” responses using legacy survey sentinel code 999 (must be replaced with np.nan before averaging or modeling!).
    • maternal_hb_g_dl has 22 missing (NaN) laboratory readings requiring leakage-free imputation inside Scikit-Learn pipelines.
    • Clustered household structure (cluster_id) mandates GroupKFold / StratifiedGroupKFold in Parts V and VII.

D.2.3 3. epi_surveillance.csv (416 Rows, 8 Columns)

  • Domain: Weekly district-level infectious disease surveillance and meteorological panel (\(4\text{ districts} \times 2\text{ years [2024–2025]} \times 52\text{ epi-weeks} = 416\text{ rows}\)).
  • Exact Columns: year, epi_week (1–52), district (Dhaka, Chattogram, Sylhet, Rajshahi), population, rainfall_mm, mean_temp_c, dengue_cases, respiratory_cases.
  • Data Quality & Quirks: Zero missing values; displays strong seasonal monsoon peaks, lagged rainfall-to-dengue transmission, and cross-district population scale differences (requiring incidence normalization per \(100{,}000\) population, panel-safe .groupby("district").shift(), TimeSeriesSplit, and GRU/LSTM sequence windowing in Chapter 31).
